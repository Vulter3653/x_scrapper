# H1 Exploratory Regression Memo

**Date:** 2026-06-18
**Status:** EXPLORATORY — NOT confirmatory evidence
**Classifier:** batch1-only provisional (OOF AUC=0.7811, FH F1=0.4770)

---

## ⚠ Critical Framing

This is an **exploratory regression**. It does not confirm H1.

The humor presence label (predictor) is generated by a **provisional batch1-only classifier**,
not gold-standard human labels for the full corpus. The classifier has:
- OOF AUC = 0.7811 (provisionally passes AUC threshold)
- Firm-held-out F1 = 0.4770 (limited cross-firm generalizability)
- Training: 1,482 batch1 human-coded posts only

Measurement error in the classifier-assigned humor label will attenuate true effects toward zero.
The observed coefficient is likely a lower bound of the true humor-engagement association.

---

## Research Design

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| DV | log_total_engagement (pre-computed in post master) |
| Main predictor | h1_humor_presence_pred_t50 (binary: 1 if prob ≥ 0.50) |
| Controls | text_length, hashtag_count, mention_count |
| Fixed effects | Firm FEs (97 firms) + Month FEs (130 months) |
| Method | OLS, two-way FE via iterative Gauss-Seidel demeaning |
| Standard errors | Standard OLS |
| N observations | 65,245 posts |
| Robustness | t40, t60, probability; DV winsorized 99%/95%; DV trimmed 99%/95% |

## Main Result

**DV: log_total_engagement | Predictor: h1_humor_presence_pred_t50**
**FE: firm + month**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Coefficient (β) | **0.2253** |
| SE | 0.0117 |
| t-statistic | 19.23 |
| p-value | < 0.001 |
| Significant at 5% | **yes** |
| Significant at 1% | **yes** |
| N obs | 65,245 |
| R² (within) | 0.0232 |

**Interpretation (exploratory):**
Humor-classified posts (t50) are associated with approximately exp(0.225) − 1 ≈ **25.2% higher**
total engagement compared to non-humor posts, after controlling for text length, hashtag count,
mention count, and absorbing firm-level and month-level fixed effects.

This association is statistically significant in the exploratory sample. It is NOT a causal estimate.
With n=65,245 and firm+month FE, statistical power is very high; significance does not imply
substantive importance or confirmatory evidence for H1.

humor rates). The t50 result is the most interpretable as the balanced-threshold main specification.

- "In the exploratory H1 regression (provisional classifier), humor presence (t50) is positively
  associated with log total engagement (β=0.225, SE=0.012, p<0.001) after firm and month FEs."
- "The positive association is consistent across threshold and DV robustness checks."

## Limitations

1. **Provisional classifier:** batch1-only, not validated against full corpus ground truth.
   Firm-held-out F1=0.4770 limits cross-firm claims.

2. **Large n / high power:** With 65,245 observations, even noise in the classifier label
   can produce statistically significant coefficients. The high t-statistic is partly a function
   of sample size, not just effect strength.

3. **Measurement error attenuation:** Classifier noise attenuates estimated β toward zero.
   True effect (if real) may be larger than 0.225.

4. **Within-firm confounders:** Firm and month FEs remove between-firm and between-month
   variation, but within-firm confounders (e.g., campaign intensity, product launches) may
   simultaneously drive humor AND engagement.

5. **Classifier-engagement correlation:** The classifier was trained on stratified batch1 posts.
   If high-engagement posts systematically look more "humorous" in text features, classifier
   predictions may be partly driven by engagement-correlated text features, inflating the coefficient.

6. **No causal identification:** This is a cross-sectional observational association within
   firm-month cells. No exogenous variation in humor is exploited.
